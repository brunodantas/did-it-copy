The KDE objection

"it would get really annoying to see big chunky system notifications appearing and covering up content in your windows all the time in response to a very common action"50

Nate Graham, KDE, 24 March 2024
  1. 24 March 2024. A KDE Discuss thread asks for a small "Copied 1 file" notification. Nate Graham suggests the tray icon could shake instead50.
  2. 6 January 2026. Nate closes the matching bug as WONTFIX, citing the KDE HIG5152.
  3. 20 June 2026. GNOME's design team opens "Clipboard feedback & history", whose first goal is "Show visible feedback when copying"53.
  4. 13 August 2026. Nate confirms a new KDE request and retitles it "Show OSD after copy"54.
